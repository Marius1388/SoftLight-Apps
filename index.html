Who we are

Our work

What we've built

MediLens

Available on Android

MediLens is a medication scanner and reminder app that uses advanced OCR camera technology to instantly identify medicines from their labels. Supporting 6 languages and a database of 98,000+ medicines, MediLens helps users manage their medication schedules safely and confidently — all offline.

  • 📷 OCR medicine scanner
  • 💊 98,000+ medicines
  • ⏰ Smart reminders
  • 🌍 6 languages
  • 🔒 Privacy first
  • 📴 Works offline
Get it on Google Play

SmileVillage

Live at smilevillage.ro

SmileVillage is the presentation website we designed and built for the dental practice of Dr. Roxana Dancea in Traian Vuia, Timiș. A fast single-page site that showcases the practice and its services, with a photo gallery, an interactive map, and a secure contact form for appointment requests.

  • 🦷 Dental practice website
  • 🖼️ Photo gallery
  • 🗺️ Interactive map
  • ✉️ Secure contact form
  • ⚡ Fast & lightweight
  • 📱 Responsive design
